# The White House Example

## Persona: High-Anxiety Security Perimeter

A boring 2000s Albuquerque ranch on the outside. Extreme privacy and local control on the inside. That contrast is the point.

## Why It Works as a Demo

- **Relatable:** Typical layout, typical devices, typical pain points.
- **High-stakes:** You want a system that works offline and does not phone home.
- **Maps to RyzaLab values:** Local-first, boringly reliable, fully documented.

## Use Case

Privacy-first and intrusion detection. Automations that notify you when something is off—without cloud dependencies or data leakage.

## Tagline

**Local control. No cloud. No witnesses.**

Translation: your automations run in your home, even if your internet is down. Your data stays yours. This is about privacy and reliability, not wrongdoing.

## Disclaimer

This example is for educational and marketing purposes. It illustrates privacy-first design and reliable local automation. It is not about hiding activity—it is about owning your data and keeping your home secure on your terms.

## Example Config

See `config/packages/examples/white_house/` for:

- Helpers (input_booleans, input_numbers)
- Automations (departure guard, leak alert, night path, Breaking Bad–inspired fun)
- Sonos routines

All entity IDs are placeholders. Replace them with your own before use.
